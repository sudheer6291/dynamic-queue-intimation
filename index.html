Who's looking at this?

Each role below is its own separate screen, on its own URL — the way a real deployment actually works: a receptionist gets their own terminal, a doctor their own, an administrator their own. There's no shared tab bar here to jump between roles — open the one that matches who you're standing in for.

Patient / Customer
What the person waiting sees on their own phone — their token, live ETA, and full visit journey.
Front Desk
The multi-station operator console — call next, register a new appointment, priority insert, pull-forward.
Doctor / Core station
One screen, one button — complete whoever's being served and call the next.
Admin dashboard
Station load, no-shows, slots recovered, prediction accuracy, and the what-if capacity planner.
Display board
Now serving / next up / waiting, per station — the screen on the wall.

The vertical (Hospital OPD / Car service / Bike service) and clock position are shared across every role's screen once you pick one — switch stations with each screen's own "All role screens" link.
